# Target player and scope

## Primary user

A serious improver who has crossed the “hang fewer pieces” stage and now loses through:

- plan selection;
- move order;
- timing;
- structure;
- attack/counterplay races;
- transition and conversion.

## Initial difficulty band

Approximate online rapid range: 1400–2200.

This is not a hard gate. Packs declare prerequisites and difficulty. A 1200 can use a guided rook ending; a titled player can use a deeply authored opening race.

## Excluded from v0

- beginner move rules and basic tactics curriculum;
- full repertoire construction;
- automatic personal coaching;
- native tournament server;
- chat-based general chess assistant;
- mobile-native clients;
- comprehensive opening/endgame encyclopedia;
- automated master-level strategic authoring.
