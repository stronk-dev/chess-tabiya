# Research limitations

- Public product pages show claimed features, not learning effectiveness.
- Several products require payment or accounts and were not subjected to a standardized paid hands-on study in this package.
- The user's report that Chess Endgame Training is slow and has poor UX is treated as explicit field feedback, not independently benchmarked performance data.
- Forum posts are anecdotes and suffer from selection bias.
- Maia-3 benchmark quality does not prove coherent long-horizon strategy.
- The 2026 deliberate-practice study is observational and its population skews below the target level; it supports the training design but does not validate this product.
- Exact commercial licensing implications of AGPL/GPL components require legal review.
- No claim is made that the proposed branch metrics fully capture chess initiative or strategic quality.
- The example drill pack is a schema fixture, not reviewed chess instruction.

## UX observations supplied by the project owner

The statement that Chess Endgame Training feels slow and has poor UX is treated as a direct user observation, not as an independently benchmarked fact. Phase 0 therefore includes a timed, repeatable teardown on the target hardware before using that weakness in public positioning.
