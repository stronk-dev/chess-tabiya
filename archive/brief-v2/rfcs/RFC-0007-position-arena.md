# RFC-0007 — Position Arena

## Status

Deferred proposal.

## Initial scope

External Lichess challenge integration, two-leg color-swapped match, PGN re-import, branch comparison.

## Native scope later

Authoritative clocks, reconnect, anti-cheat, matchmaking, moderation and separate training rating.

## Match contract

- exact pack/version/root;
- time control;
- two legs;
- color assignments;
- objective visibility;
- result and PGN;
- review session link.
