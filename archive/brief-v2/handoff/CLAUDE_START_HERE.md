## Workstreams

### A — competitor hands-on teardown

For each closest product, execute standardized tasks and record actual behavior rather than landing-page claims.

### B — concept taxonomy

Refine the v1 mistake/structure taxonomy with a strong chess reviewer. Keep categories observable and drillable.

### C — first pack authoring

Produce:

- one Sicilian move-order/timing pack;
- one quiet structure pack;
- one rook-endgame pack.

Every claim must cite engine/tablebase/corpus/author evidence and pass review.

### D — feedback language

Write concise templates that explain objective, timing and structural divergence. Avoid best-move worship and generic strategic prose.

### E — evaluation protocol

Turn `14_VALIDATION_AND_KILL_CRITERIA.md` into a preregistered small study with baseline conditions.

## Deliverables

- reviewed pack JSONs;
- source PGNs/FENs with provenance;
- reviewer notes;
- feedback templates;
- unresolved chess disagreements;
- competitor teardown report;
- no invented product claims.
