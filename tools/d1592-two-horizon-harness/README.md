# D1592–D1597 campaign two-horizon harness

Disposable Node-24 research instrument. It does not implement campaign persistence or select
product copy. It exercises the contract seams returned from `campaign-core`:

- server-authoritative reward namespaces actually present at HEAD;
- owned / equipped / resting / encounter-suppressed / effective inventory separation;
- path-sensitive acquisition → later consumer and acquisition → boss-consumer checks;
- completion-denominator prestige;
- event-owned abandonment and a distinct completed cursor; and
- idempotent cross-run awards keyed by learner, campaign version, run and reward.

Run:

```sh
/opt/homebrew/opt/node@24/bin/node --test tools/d1592-two-horizon-harness/model.test.mjs
```

The path checks prove structural opportunity only. They do not claim a reward caused success or
that a loadout feels fun. The durable-reward census deliberately reports browser-only appearance
catalogs and draft-only theory identities as unavailable to a server-owned campaign at HEAD.
