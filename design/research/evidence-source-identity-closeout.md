# Evidence source-identity closeout for the declared 1.0 basis

**Question.** After the exact-identity and provider-contract audits, is any requested 1.0 evidence
family still an unowned semantic-research gap, and which apparent absences are instead unlanded
sources, version repairs, operations, validation or consumers?

**Verdict.** The declared 1.0 source basis is research-closed but not implemented. Thirty
capability families now have a traceable semantic authority: **14 have a landed source boundary,
7 require a versioned repair to an existing source, and 9 have a specified source contract that is
not landed**. No row remains an unowned collector-semantics question. `[V]`

That is not a declaration that evidence, Support, Review, bots, drills, style or variants are
complete. Sixteen of the thirty source families still require production work, and even a sound
source remains unusable until a real operation emits it, independent fixtures can falsify it, a
consumer admits it, a reducer selects it and a workflow presents it. The source closeout removes
ontology uncertainty from the critical path; it does not collapse those later obligations.

## Method and scope

The executable receipt at `tools/d1737-source-identity-closeout/` composes the repository's prior
set-equal instruments instead of repeating their measurements. It binds:

- all **37 producer roots / 193 compiled projections**;
- all **18** structural-reading families and **14** transition-reading leaves;
- the closed **30** tactical and **18** breadth collector inventories;
- all **67** semantic-event projections;
- thirty required source-capability families reconstructed from the owner's examples, D717/Waves
  A–C, R21's player-style atoms and the roadmap's variant evidence obligation;
- all seventeen source-program author handoffs and the bounded-target/Review readers. `[V]`

The receipt is deliberately not a universal chess ontology. New theory and concepts remain
extensible. Its claim is narrower: every evidence family already required for the recorded 1.0
vision has a semantic basis and a production disposition, so new work cannot hide behind “we may
need another classifier” without naming a new learner question and passing the exploration gate.

## What changed from the 2026-08-24 closure

`foundation-capability-closure.md` was directionally right but is no longer a current source
receipt. It called non-mate 2–3-ply prevention the sole new collector-level research gap. D1023
subsequently closed that question by splitting exact named-target removal/return from depth-labelled
Stockfish and band-labelled Maia policy facts. The later contract pass split production into local
target derivation, reusable provider receipts/scheduling and target-policy composition. `[V]`
(`bounded-policy-targets.md`; `bounded-policy-target-contract-closure.md`)

The earlier closure also called several source families “shipped” at too coarse a grain. Later
fixed-population audits proved source-identity defects in king opposition, backward pawns,
isolated/doubled groups, pawn-island events, slider-ray membership and named structures, and found
the missing stationary heavy-piece file-access event. These are versioned repairs, not reasons to
throw away the broader foundation. `[V]`

Finally, the old matrix did not include the literal source atoms required by the already-measured
player-style lane or the rules-bearing evidence boundary required by 1.0 variants. Both are now
first-class rows. Style categories themselves do **not** become source facts: natural archetypes
failed their stability gate, and the retained continuous metrics still need longitudinal and
time-control validation before learner cards become production defaults. `[V]`
(`player-style-metrics.md`; `longitudinal-style-feedback-contract.md`;
`variant-evidence-operation-boundary.md`)

## Source closure is only stage one

The remaining chain is intentionally explicit:

| layer | current blocking evidence | required result |
|---|---|---|
| source identity/provenance | 7 version repairs + 9 unlanded sources | one versioned payload authority and transitive source metadata |
| application operation | D1710 found all 67 semantic events stop before live application operations | packet/path/provider operations emit sealed items under bounded cost and honest absence |
| independent validation | D1711/D1713 found generated labels and incomplete hard-negative authority | independent positive, negative, abstention and population receipts |
| counterfactual denominator | D1716/D1719 lose subjects and same-move events | subject-first distinct-move denominators over exact identities |
| module selection | D1726 found rich sources behind zero ordinary consumers | question-bound module accepts/reducers; raw inventories Advanced-only |
| workflow/UX | presets and board composition remain downstream | stable board plus named modules, defaults and guided flows rather than evidence strings |
| Review/bots/style/drills | shared facts exist at uneven reach | all consumers bind the same ids through their own eligibility and selection policies |

This prevents the two recurring category errors: commissioning a duplicate detector because an
operation or consumer is absent, and claiming a capability complete because a projection appears
in the catalogue. `[V]`

## Execution ownership for the sixteen non-landed families

The source-research receipt initially named all sixteen production gaps but implied the D1736 wave
owned the entire set. The executable owner join now assigns exactly one living destination: `[V]`

- **twelve** version/source/provider families route through D1736's shared amendment wave;

This is ownership, not landing permission. Draft/returned owners still require amendment,
independent review and acceptance before implementation.

## D1738: the consolidation itself was not closed

The D1736 wave said every handoff must be referenced once but named only six of its original
sixteen program handoffs by filename; ten appeared as topic shorthand. Its bounded-policy reader
lived in another directory and was mentioned without a path. The closeout replaced shorthand with
a literal inventory plus two downstream readers; D1739 then added the missing style-atom handoff,
bringing the checked program inventory to seventeen. The harness now fails on an omitted,
additional or multiply referenced handoff. `[V]`

## Decision and implementation order

1. Author and accept D1736's shared source-identity/provider sequence, consuming all seventeen
   literal handoffs; do not split shared compiler/manifest work by chess noun.
2. In parallel, amend/review the three sibling owners: bounded targets, theory and variants. Land
   the seven version repairs and nine new source boundaries with stable old pack predicates or
   explicit migrations.
3. Bind real packet/path/provider operations and independent validation before learner eligibility.
4. Implement subject-safe avoidance, then migrate ordinary modules away from legacy inventories.
5. Only then finish presets/composition and connect Review, bots, theory, drills and longitudinal
   readers. Each remains responsible for significance and display, not source chess truth.

Content expansion still waits on the stable capability/version boundary. Adding an optional
projection must not force pack edits; changing an authored predicate's meaning requires an explicit
migration and human semantic review.

## Limits

- `[V]` This pass validates inventory, authorities and architectural boundaries. It does not rerun
  every underlying chess population; it composes their immutable/committed receipts.
- `[V]` A landed source is not necessarily learner-eligible or independently validated.
- `[V]` Player-style longitudinal transfer across weeks/time controls remains a measurement gap;
  that is a product-validity gate, not an unowned chess-source definition.
- `[M]` Future chess concepts are expected. A proposed addition must identify a learner question
  not expressible as a join over current atoms, rather than growing a universal classifier dump.
