# Graduation clearance — bounded author repair

**Date:** 2026-09-07

**Scope:** [[D3088]]–[[D3091]] only

**Lifecycle result:** author repair complete; the RFC remains returned/implementing until a genuinely fresh review accepts the schema-0.28 amendment.

## What changed

- One disposable evaluator model executes the real nine `emitterGraduationBlocker` shapes through
  false → minimum truthful mutation → true → decided-input mutation → false.
- Evaluation rejoins each blocking `entry.id` to the closed template plan and rejects altered,
  omitted or extra persisted plan fields before considering its predicate.
- The unsupported `author_attested` actor claim is removed. `content_declared` records a
  repository-governed declaration over exact bytes; it does not authenticate a person or chess
  truth. `declaredAt` is self-declared but included in `declarationDigest`, so it cannot be rewritten
  without invalidating the declaration.
- The planner's private canonicalizer is deleted. Planner and evaluator hash the exact bytes emitted
  by `packages/schema/src/drill-pack/digest.ts#canonicalizeJson`, including its malformed-Unicode
  refusal domain.

## Executable receipt

`make graduation-clearance-author-repair` passes seven groups:

1. nine real emitter shapes, 9/9 false → 9/9 true → 9/9 stale-false;
2. every persisted plan field immutable and extra caller-selected pointers refused;
3. all three dangerous-direction controls;
4. matching declarations cannot clear empty authored collections or an empty recorded spine;
5. `win`, `hold`, `save`, and `resist` all use the production `objectiveRules` compiler and require grading;
6. declaration identity/time/payload staleness and authority-label controls; and
7. shared RFC-8785 lone-surrogate refusals.

`make graduation-plan-check` passes ten groups and confirms the checked-in proposal remains total:
436 entries, 204 mechanically ready, 232 author-required, zero contract-blocked.

## Boundary

No pack schema, corpus document, sidecar, production clearance evaluator, protected design intent or
official content changed. The disposable model is acceptance evidence, not a second production
implementation; schema 0.28 must replace it with direct production tests in its implementing commit.
