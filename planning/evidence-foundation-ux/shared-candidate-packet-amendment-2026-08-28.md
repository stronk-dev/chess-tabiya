## Provider join repaired in parallel

[[D1943]] pins legal-root scores to `root_side_to_move` centipawns or categorical
`root_mates|root_is_mated` move distance. [[D1944]] makes the complete
`ProviderEvidenceDelivery<T>`—not bare `T`—the F1 source payload, retaining the acquisition receipt,
delivery kind, cache identity and actual generation. These are amendments to the still-draft
provider RFC; they do not make provider execution an implementation dependency of the packet's
provider-free first landing.

6. Re-run the production-call census, memory envelope and normal repository gates against the
