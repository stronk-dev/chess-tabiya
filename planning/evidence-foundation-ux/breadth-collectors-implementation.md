**RFC:** `rfc/breadth-collectors.md`  
**State:** accepted; queued immediately behind complete tactical-collectors landing  
**Scope:** 18 exact research/inspector projection ids; no modules, prose, presets, schemas,
content, player types or bot personalities.

## Entry condition

Do not begin production edits until tactical-collectors has landed all 30 identities and closed its
ledger/log lifecycle. This wave consumes the single compiled `legal_exchange@1`, capture,
passed-pawn, check and `reply_breadth@1` authorities. It must not recreate any of them.

At 2026-08-22 HEAD, all 18 Appendix-A ids are absent from `evidence-catalog.ts`; this is the full
wave, not a partial repair.

## Execution slices

### B1 — square control and mobility

- Add `square-control.ts`: pseudo-controller sets remain available under current occupancy;
  complete legal-controller sets use one disclosed per-color turn clone and typed whole-set
  abstention.
- Add `mobility.ts`: per B/N/R/Q identity, exact legal destinations and the locally-non-losing
  subset using the already-landed exchange authority.

- Pin opposite-check clone failure, absolute pins, pawn empty diagonals, friendly occupancy,
  legal captures, safe/unsafe destinations and color mirrors.

### B2 — pawn state, events, and retained sequences

  contact creation remains the existing transition authority.
- Compile recorded two-edge and three-edge contact/harassment sequences with byte-equal shared
  anchors and retained pawn/minor/slider/screen/target identities.
- Pin all six projection ids with the RFC's equality, rank, horizon and replacement-identity hard
  negatives.

### B3 — defender exposure and recorded consequence

- Consume square-control's exact pseudo-controller delta, not the older zero/nonzero aggregate.
- Join a lost defender edge to the exact retained target and a positive local exchange.
- Compile the recorded three-edge consequence without naming removal, deflection, overload,
  forcing or success; those semantic names belong to the accepted successor RFC.
- Retain the authored-corpus zero and canonical positive/disagreement fixtures.

### B4 — material, king, and open-file activity operands

- Add `material-state.ts`: derive P/N/B/R/Q role vectors from the existing piece-count authority;

  consume square-control rather than recomputing attacks.
- Join captured-piece identity to its prior zone-defender role, including the exact en-passant
  captured square.
- Compile open/half-open-file occupancy as a moved heavy-piece event using the existing file-class
  reading; stationary-piece class changes remain negative.

### B5 — manifest closure, measurement, and lifecycle

- Register the eight producer ids and all 18 closed projection ids with exact Appendix-A metadata.
- Add brand-sealed adapters and only required public exports.
- Compile every event through the F2 event authority; no raw payload or second enumerator.
- Reproduce authored/imported measurements separately and retain zeroes/domain corrections.
- Update only the two declared evidence docs, then close D802–D807 and append the exploration log
