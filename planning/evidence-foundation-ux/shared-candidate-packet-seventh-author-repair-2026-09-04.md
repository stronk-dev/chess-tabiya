## Outcome

The common candidate population remains the right denominator for Support, Review and bot policy,
but the sixth draft had three authority gaps. The seventh repair closes them without widening the
feature claim.

### One value factory, one owner

`evidence-value-authority` owns and lands
`createRulesMobilityReadingLegalMovesV1Evidence`. The packet imports that exact symbol. It no longer
claims the predecessor-deleted adapter or any change to `evidence-factories.ts`. Its source-graph
criterion rejects recreating the adapter, introducing a wrapper alias, defining the factory again,
or importing either legal enumerator beneath the factory boundary.

### Scope controls work, not truth

`CandidateCollectorContext` no longer contains request scope. The planner uses scope to select a
dependency-closed execution plan and the executor uses it to retain outputs; neither exposes it to
chess computation. Direct narrow and projected wide paths must agree on projection/value digest,
abstention and outcome tuples for every shared family. An in-service projection additionally keeps
the exact references.

### The cache bounds what it actually retains

The `events + 5×readings` coefficient and inherited `8 / 56,000` defaults are withdrawn. The private
receipt-reference authority now feeds a complete graph walker over the packet, legal declaration and
moves, candidate rows, visible evidence, abstentions, retained outcomes and hidden dependency
outcomes. It records canonical logical UTF-8 bytes, unique objects and a closed category census,
deduplicating only reference-identical objects. The service requires explicit logical-byte and
object-count limits until the exact production graph is calibrated under Node 24.

The model proves why the distinction matters: a quiet packet with no visible evidence still has a
non-zero retained graph; adding hidden outcomes increases both measures; sharing one object counts it
once; replacing that shared reference with an equal clone increases the object and byte totals.

## Remaining gate

This is positive author evidence, not acceptance. A new reviewer must attack the repaired factory
ownership, scope-equivalence relation and complete-graph traversal independently. Production
implementation remains unauthorized until that review passes and the predecessor has landed.
