## Deviations recorded in the RFC changelog

1. The predecessor factory was missing. Its pinned route landed minimally in `evidence-source-adapters.ts`.
2. The closure has 44 one-edge event identities, not 47: `.sequence.` projections are excluded.
3. `legalAlternativeEdges` stays independent, as the oracle for criterion 5.
4. The loose-piece abstention is now typed, and it propagates to `counterfactual_population_incomplete`.
5. The selection tests were rewritten against receipts.
6. The compile-failure algebra is minimal, and `SealedCandidateCollectorOutcome` is minimal (D11/D12 own the rest).
