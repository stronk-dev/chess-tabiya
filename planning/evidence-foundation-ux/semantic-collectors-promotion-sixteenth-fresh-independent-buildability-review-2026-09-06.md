# Semantic collectors promotion — sixteenth fresh independent buildability review

- Date: 2026-09-06
- Reviewed: `rfc/semantic-collectors.md` §3.7 and the fifteenth author model
- Verdict: **RETURNED** on [[D3018]]–[[D3024]]
- Executable receipt: `make semantic-collectors-promotion-sixteenth-fresh-review`
- Production effect: none; the evidence spine remains 12/14 and both promotion projections remain
  unauthorized

## What survived

The fifteenth repair closes its narrow installation defects. Inventory and generation documents
have exact grammars; all listed artifacts are opened before registry publication; pack, sourcing,
Syzygy-response and legal-map validators execute; one application, registry and store are
single-flighted; copied application/store/request/result objects fail their local runtime seals.
Those gains remain retained by the new target.

## Fresh returns

1. **[[D3018]] configuration is still caller authority.** The exported composition factory accepts
   any caller-selected `promotionInstallationPath`. Two unrelated temporary installations both
   become fully accepted applications and registries. A closure around a raw path does not make the
   path product configuration.
2. **[[D3019]] geometry and the no-race gate disappeared.** The model contains no contacts input,
   geometry derivation, completion assertion or completed/no-evidence arm. It emits a tablebase
   reading for the RFC's own a2-versus-b7 hard negative, where both contact rows are `passed: false`
   and §3.7 requires `no_opposing_passed_clear_paths`.
3. **[[D3020]] the collector ABI is a different operation.** The normative request is
   `{geometry, providerScope, signal}` plus recorded/legal/provider dependencies. The model exposes
   `{store, fen, signal}`, accepts no dependencies, and has no recorded-first/live-Syzygy/domain
   source selection, scheduler digest, provider invocation or success-only legal resolution. The
   strict TypeScript control proves the normative calls are unrepresentable.
4. **[[D3021]] the output loses required operands.** `geometry` and `promotionFirst` are absent, and
   `immediatePromotion` is every legal promotion rather than the exact participant join. Generic
   `declareEvidence` replaces the sole registered derived-value constructor and its receipt.
5. **[[D3022]] recorded authority is re-created locally.** The store calls generic
   `declareEvidence` and installs a local WeakMap receipt instead of consuming the central
   sourcing-ledger and recorded-tablebase factory chain that §3.7 and D6 require. The missing
   dependency is a blocker, not permission to emulate it.
6. **[[D3023]] durable-record closure regressed.** Validation uses `find` for one matching record
   and never proves uniqueness or set equality. A generation with two byte-identical tablebase
   records publishes and the collector silently chooses the first, contradicting C18 and the prior
   [[D2839]] repair.
7. **[[D3024]] the result algebra drifted.** The model invents `cancelled`, maps recorded absence
   directly to `provider_unavailable`, and cannot retain the RFC's completed/no-race or
   outside-domain result authorities. Local sealing cannot repair a missing semantic state.

## Buildability consequence

The installation parser is usable work, but it is not yet the held promotion pair's buildable
contract. The next bounded repair must compose that installation authority with the already
specified geometry and tablebase operation instead of replacing them. It must also bind
configuration to the actual product composition, restore the durable-record bijection, and wait
for the central value/provider authorities rather than minting local substitutes. Another genuinely
fresh review still precedes implementation.
