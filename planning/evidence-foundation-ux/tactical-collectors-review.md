## Evidence verdict

The prerequisite is supported, with bounded claims:

- 39,755 played/alternative edges across independent authored and sealed imported populations;
- deterministic paired source-position bootstrap, 2,000 resamples;
- legal, X-ray, pinned-recapturer and geometry-disagreement controls;
- negative-primary en-prise result in both populations with both intervals below 1;
- exchange-filtered fork point estimate above 1 in both populations, but only the imported
  interval excludes 1.
- exact reply enumeration over another 38,461 legal alternatives at about .26/.39 ms per
  candidate edge in the disposable harness;
- generic threat presence near background and all-reply consequences proven rare rather
  than assumed useful.

The result does **not** make Wave A a complete middlegame ontology. Phase 2b is now complete and its
admitted families are specified separately in `rfc/breadth-collectors.md` (D802), rather than hidden
as future research or inflated into this RFC. An independent reviewer should reject this RFC if it
turns local exchange/reply breadth into move quality, force, strategic intent, a universal ranking
prior or a substitute for the breadth successor.

## Independent review checklist

1. Recompute Appendix A's 30-id count and compare every id with its §3 semantics.
2. Trace each derivation input; especially capture→trade and fork state→reply consequence.
3. Attempt pinned-recapturer, illegal-king, promotion and en-passant counterexamples.
4. Verify `threat@1` cannot construct an illegal pass state.
5. Verify no production edit outside §1.1 is required; if one is, return the RFC.
6. Verify D743/opening identity is absent from implementation acceptance, not merely optional.
7. Verify A7/A9 preserve the authored uncertainty and cannot pass by weakening the measurement.
8. Verify no module, preset, workflow or learner sentence is introduced in the collector landing.
9. Prove `reply_breadth@1` is set-equal to the legal move generator and every consequence consumes
   that compiled object rather than a second enumerator.
10. Verify D794's zero authored fork-consequence population remains recorded and does not weaken A5.

Only after that review may the owner accept the RFC and open implementation. The breadth successor
can be reviewed in parallel but cannot implement before this RFC supplies its legal-exchange/reply
prerequisites.
