## What survives

The maintained sixth target really does transitively execute all five earlier author generations.
The RFC prose also preserves the important product boundary: race geometry remains useful without
an outcome, Syzygy is the only outcome authority, and the two held projections remain unimplemented.

## Return

The new executable model is not the operation specified by the RFC. Its request drops geometry,
provider scope and abort signal; its provider invocation drops the typed result/delivery. Its
advertised F1 output is a plain `{payload}` object with no producer, projection or central
value-authority receipt.

Within that shadow model, the new seals are green by construction. Public helpers mint “exact”
legal-map and geometry receipts from caller arrays, so a newly minted same-FEN receipt admits a
dropped underpromotion and invented empty ordering. Tablebase source constructors accept unparsed
outcomes—including an invented category/perspective and `NaN`—and the model's four-category union
does not cover the real ten-category server authority. Finally, total-result constructors accept
bare mutable objects for local-domain, provider-failure and no-evidence arms; mutating those nested
values after construction changes a result its WeakSet assertion still accepts.

These are not production bugs because the held pair has not landed. They are buildability failures:
the author evidence does not yet exercise the exact provider/value-authority inputs, declared F1
output or total result promised by the RFC.

## Next

Repair the model against the real request/result and declared-evidence types, consume independently
minted specialized source receipts rather than author-created tokens, make every total arm retain
only exact asserted immutable inputs, and cover the complete tablebase category/number domain.
Then run another genuinely fresh review. The original twelve collectors remain implemented and
unchanged; the held promotion pair remains unauthorized.
