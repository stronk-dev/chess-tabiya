- closes run rewards at module, exact theory passage and campaign-rewind resource grants;

  catalog cosmetics, with export/delete/restore/merge/backup obligations; and
- makes the 1.0 UX boundary a complete route journey rather than a backend reducer: Campaign home,
  map, preparation, stable in-run context, node/run results and Review return.

`make campaign-two-horizon-author-contract` composes the nine existing research-model arms with ten
checks over the amended normative RFC and shared registers.
