## Existing evidence and rulings

- Boss: `design/06-campaign.md` §§2b/5, `design/research/training-mode-variants.md`,
  `planning/campaign/rfc-derivation.md`, and `rfc/learner-rating.md` §5.3a already establish that the
  boss is a full `position` game, only Act II may be rated, and rating is admitted only when clean.
- Catalogue: [[D1151]] is the owner ruling; [[D300]] supplies the bounded vocabulary gap. Its
  projection must remain a catalogue fact and never become a claim about learner skill.
- Durable variety: `design/research/campaign-two-horizon-contract.md` establishes the valid minimum
  (marks plus shared-catalog cosmetics) and refuses generic ids. [[D2251]] records that this minimum
  is not consequential; a dedicated comparative research pass and owner ruling remain mandatory.
- Official curriculum: `campaign-core.md` owns the typed metadata and validator. Human chess review
  remains law-8 content authority and cannot be discharged mechanically.

## Dependency order

The foundation may be reviewed while dependencies are repaired, but implementation waits for the
accepted provider/module/theory/preset/bot/store authorities it imports. Boss drafting can proceed
from existing research, but implementation waits for bot policy and learner rating. Catalogue work
waits for the evidence vocabulary authority so authored packs are not repeatedly rewritten. Durable
variety research is independent and should run while those implementation dependencies clear.

The roadmap checker must treat these as one Campaign capability: the foundation can become
implemented while Campaign remains incomplete. No row may use “v2,” “later” or a discharge without
this exact owner and receipt.
