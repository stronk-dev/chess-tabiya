1. a compiled, versioned producer→projection→consumer manifest is the evidence authority;
2. semantic eligibility precedes local selection, module composition and rendering;
3. ordinary learners choose an opinionated workflow/preset, while every primitive still has an
   explicit advanced/inspector/author/operator disposition;
4. session/workflow ceilings can only remove assistance;
5. deterministic rendering is normative and the LLM is optional post-selection wording;
6. 1.0 targets the owner-ruled Choice-C self-hostable appliance as well as a supported hosted
   deployment; and
7. old “shipped” breadth claims are corrected to current integration truth.

The pass must **not** choose exact learner-facing detector families, preset names/budgets/defaults,
Review Map moments, theory workflow, campaign scope, coach/streamer defaults, or participant
accessibility success. Those remain with their named research/decision nodes.

### `design/04-content-architecture.md`

Add the O1/Gate-F relationship near **What is actually primitive / production model**:

- a reusable primitive is not merely a schema enum or shape trigger; it declares its producer,
  typed projection(s), operands, grounding/exactness/abstention, consumers and compatibility;
- predicates, inspector readings and learner events may project differently and therefore need
  distinct semantic identities/versions;
- pack capability requirements and semantic dependencies are machine-readable, so migrations find
  indirect users such as `outpost`→`pawn_safe_square`;
- adding a producer does not require rewriting unrelated packs; a pack changes only when it claims
  that capability or depends on changed semantics;
- the D560 scale-content hold remains until Gate F, a dependency-aware dry-run and a
  primitive-complete sacrificial official pilot pass.

Do not select the 1.0 theory source or stable primitive set here; O5/O6 remain blocked by R8/R10.

### `design/05-in-run-experience.md`

Amend **The assistance ladder** without discarding the useful source taxonomy:

- rung 0 arithmetic may be exact within declared scope, but a detector name, projection or learner
  interpretation is not automatically exact; cite A3's operand loss/projection mismatch as the
  reason eligibility is separate;
- the ladder describes source risk, not producer/consumer wiring and not significance;
- learner events require typed subject/object/squares, sign, grounding, exactness/confidence,
  abstention and positive/hard-negative validation;
- local distinctiveness/rarity can select among already-eligible events but cannot establish
  valence, causality, importance or a move grade.

Amend **Presentation/config**:

- form remains orthogonal to source honesty, but a producer may use only forms declared by the
  manifest; “any rung in any form” is not implicit permission;
- store workflow identity and requested preset separately from technical source preferences;
- effective assistance is `requested preset ∩ workflow/session ceiling ∩ honesty/access ∩ source
  availability`; every term only narrows;
- ordinary views expose modules/presets; raw source/form switches remain Advanced/Custom or the
  inspector; every primitive still has an explicit disposition somewhere;
- exact requested sight is legal pre-commit; proactive blunder prevention belongs only to an
  explicit Support preset and is not the rehearsal default;
- theory-only, honest-empty and source-unavailable are first-class states;
- input semantics must be equivalent for touch, hover/pointer and keyboard/assistive use.

Amend **Guided mode** so it is a module composition, not “the shape library rendered live.” Shapes
may ground a structure/theory nudge, while threat radar, safe-move warning, legal affordance and
compare/review modules use different eligible producers. No module may dump the raw evidence pool.

Amend **LLM is the voice** with R5's measured boundary:

- deterministic rendering is normative and the provider-off fallback;
- the LLM receives only selected evidence and may vary tone/brevity after deterministic
  provenance/citations are fixed outside its output;
- it never selects evidence, grades moves, adds theory, chooses a rung/preset, or raises assistance;
- a schema-valid model response is not proof of citation preservation; each provider/model version
  must pass a conformance set or fall back.

Update **Open question 1** as partially ruled: requested exact sight may be available pre-commit;
proactive prevention requires explicit Support. Preserve the R3 question of exact default
composition/budgets. Update **Open question 4**: raw per-context config is no longer the ordinary
default model; workflows have product opinions/presets and advanced controls remain configurable.
