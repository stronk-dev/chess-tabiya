moment into a relevant consequence-playing drill—and back—without duplicating chess truth or
inventing applicability?

## Automatic failures

- semantic similarity alone declares theory or a drill applicable;
- a prospective shape reference is offered as present practice;
- a link drops the exact pack/run/node identity before launch;
- theory prose is copied into a pack instead of referenced;
- a Review card promises theory/drill where no exact join exists;
- an LLM chooses the join or writes the underlying chess claim;
- an empty result is replaced with a generic lesson or engine line.
