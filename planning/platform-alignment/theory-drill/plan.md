# R8 theory↔drill workflow — research plan

**Status:** work units 1–4 mechanically complete 2026-08-21; owner use and O5/O6 handoff remain

## Question

Can a learner move from a detected structure, opening identity, grounded explanation or Review
moment into a relevant consequence-playing drill—and back—without duplicating chess truth or
inventing applicability?

## Work units

1. **DONE.** Census authored shape/principle/opening identities and trace every current learner edge.
2. **DONE.** Compare current theory↔practice workflows in relevant products using current first-party sources;
   separate navigation patterns from claims of learning effectiveness.
3. **DONE.** Build a disposable fixed-position join prototype over exact identities only: shape firing,
   opening/transposition key where recorded, principle/claim ID and pack reference.
4. **DONE mechanically.** Exercise lookup→rehearse and Review→rehearse, including transposition
   identity, source preservation and honest no-pack/candidate-only cases. Owner use remains.
5. **NEXT.** Publish the O5/O6/F7 choice set. Do not draft F7 before the owner rules the theory source,
   applicability contract and stable primitive/re-authoring budget.

## Automatic failures

- semantic similarity alone declares theory or a drill applicable;
- a prospective shape reference is offered as present practice;
- a link drops the exact pack/run/node identity before launch;
- theory prose is copied into a pack instead of referenced;
- a Review card promises theory/drill where no exact join exists;
- an LLM chooses the join or writes the underlying chess claim;
- an empty result is replaced with a generic lesson or engine line.
