# O7 owner handoff — Review Map product contract

**Status:** ready for owner ruling  
**Inputs:** R7 mechanical/desk research, F1/F2, D686–D691  
**Does not authorize:** F6 drafting until the choices below are ruled

## What research has settled

These are constraints, not choices:

1. A Review moment is an **actionable position**, not a sentence or an engine label. Re-entry already
   preserves the source line and the learner's new branch.
2. Selection needs two stages: module eligibility/local fact selection, then a separate whole-game
   moment selector. Cap-one per position still created 13/16 moments on long trajectories.
3. Zero moments is valid. A fixed quota would refill the map with D686's exact-but-unhelpful facts.
4. Retained engine pivots are not a universal input: consecutive evaluations reach 20/20 opening
   mainlines and 0/29 middlegame/endgame mainlines at HEAD.
5. The same compiled moment IDs and evidence must feed private Review, public share and the social
   card. Current private/public selection divergence and false engine footer are defects.
6. The LLM may phrase already-selected admitted evidence. It may not select a moment, infer its
   significance, grade the move, invent a theory join or alter provenance.

## Recommended ruling

### O7.1 — Default map size

**Recommend:** up to **three** moments, no minimum. Prefer one moment from each phase actually
represented in the game; a declared critical exact event may displace that balance. “Show more” may
open the advanced inspector but does not silently enlarge the default map.

Alternatives the owner may choose: one turning point only; or up to five. Eight is the current
mechanical ceiling, not a researched learner default.

### O7.2 — What may become a moment

**Recommend:** only a workflow-declared allow-list of exact semantic events, exact tablebase state,
authored pack/checkpoint facts, opening identity/exit where recorded, and terminal outcome. Shape
firings and engine pivots may nominate a position only when the resulting card also has an admitted
learner-facing fact and an action. Generic count deltas and raw eval shifts do not become cards merely
because they are distinctive.

Every admitted family needs a positive fixture, a hard negative and an abstention case. F5's module
eligibility may be reused, but F6 owns the stricter moment allow-list.

### O7.3 — Engine role and move labels

**Recommend:** engine evidence is **optional trigger/context**, never the default verdict. The normal
map uses factual signed language such as a structure changing, a defender disappearing, or a forced
state being reached—only where the registered operands establish that statement. It does not say
best, brilliant, good, inaccuracy, mistake or blunder.

An explicit Analyze action may reveal evaluation/PV after the learner retries or asks for it. Retry
starts with the verdict hidden. If the user later enables an engine-review preset, its grades remain
visibly engine-relative and are not allowed to replace the grounded fact/action card.

### O7.4 — Action doors

**Recommend:** every moment has **Retry from here**. Compare appears after the retry produces another
branch. Cited theory and a drill appear only on an exact registered identity join; their absence is
shown honestly, never filled by semantic search or LLM invention. Analyze is explicit and secondary.

This permits intentionally obtuse help: the default can expose only squares, a named relation or a
theory reference; a direct move/PV belongs to the explicit Analyze/reveal path, not the ordinary map.

### O7.5 — Share contract

**Recommend:** the shared recap uses the same selected moment IDs, order and provenance as the
private map, but may omit private actions or prose. Its source footer is computed from the admitted
items. A short social form may use one selected moment plus a factual game/phase arc; it may not
claim “engine evidence” when the source set does not contain it.
