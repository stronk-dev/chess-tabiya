  provenance reproduction and same-dimension embedding-model invalidation are unrepresentable in
  the current store and failed.
- The key layer produced its own safety defect: untyped `pawn` filtered rook-ending questions to
  a generic pawn-ending source. Recorded D579-D581 and narrowed the 1.0 candidate to a deterministic
  provenance compiler with typed applicability and an immutable exact/SQLite-FTS bundle.
