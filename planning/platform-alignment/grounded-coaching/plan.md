## Question

Can Tabiya's current registered events and attempt history support useful recurring-pattern cards
with exact source and replay/drill actions, without turning exposure, correlation or an LLM phrase
into a diagnosis?

This arm tests information sufficiency and contract shape. It does not decide coaching defaults,
rank a learner's needs, create a production schema or claim that a card improves learning.

## Inputs

1. R12's twelve retained literal habit metrics, their separate sample floors and refusal of natural
   archetypes.
2. R7's local→whole-game selection boundary and exact source-run/node requirement.
3. R8's exact applicability prototype and honest-empty theory/drill result.
4. Current `attempts`, `attempt_concepts`, progress metrics, shape recommendations, Review Story
   evidence and action routes.
5. F1/F2's declared evidence identities and R5's deterministic/optional-LLM renderer result.

## Candidate card contract

A mechanically admissible card must carry:

- a versioned observation or metric identity;
- a declared population/window;
- numerator **and** opportunity denominator where a rate is claimed;
- sample floor and uncertainty when the source metric defines them;
- exact contributing run/node or game/ply identities, plus the total if the UI truncates examples;
- a literal deterministic sentence; and
- zero or more actions derived only from exact registered joins: replay source, retry root, open an
  applicable pack, or open applicable theory.

Allowed assertions are literal: “appeared in 4 of 12 preserved runs,” “3 of 5 graded attempts on
this root ended stable,” or “your measured opening-surprisal row is defined over 50 games.” The
following are refused by this arm: `weakness`, `strength`, `needs work`, causal explanation,
recommended training priority, personality/archetype, or a move grade.

## Tests and falsifiers

1. **Persistence reach.** Census every current attempt/history field against the candidate card.
   Imported games, source node, evidence identity, opportunity denominator, confidence and exact
   theory/drill identity must be reported present or absent independently.
2. **Concept identity.** Count pack concept references and repeated raw identities. If persistence
   makes two occurrences of the same authored identity impossible to join, raw concept coaching is
   refused until a registry/migration exists.
3. **Source closure.** A card fails if its count cannot be traced to exact contributing records, or
   if a truncated example list omits the full population count.
4. **Denominator closure.** A count without opportunities may render as a count, never as a rate,
   tendency, avoidance or deficiency.
5. **Action closure.** A pack/theory action fails unless the exact observation carries a validated
   applicability set. Display-label similarity and free text are not joins.
6. **Renderer closure.** A deterministic renderer must express every admitted card. An optional
   LLM may only paraphrase the same typed fields and citations; it receives no authority to select,
   diagnose, grade or prescribe.
7. **Negative controls.** The prototype must refuse label-only merges, missing denominators,
   source-less counts, prospective pack references and diagnostic/advice vocabulary.

## Exit

R13's mechanical arm completes when the current topology matrix, corpus identity census and
positive/negative prototype are reproducible; every safe card has a replay or honest-empty action
result; and the dossier states exactly what O9/F9 may specify versus what still needs owner use or
later evidence.
