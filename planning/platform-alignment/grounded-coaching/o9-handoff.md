# O9 owner handoff — player profile and grounded coaching

**Status:** architecture choice ready; owner-use quality and future longitudinal validation remain  
**Inputs:** R7, R12, R13, R5, R8, O5/F7, O13/F12

## Recommended ruling

1. **Ship continuous literal habit cards, not archetypes.** Each metric has its own version,
   denominator, population/window, minimum sample, uncertainty and contributing games. No single
   profile-confidence badge; no “GM twin,” tactical/positional type or personality score.
2. **Approve a versioned personal-observation ledger for F9.** It projects already-declared
   evidence and habit rows with occurrence/opportunity, exact source position and exact
   applicability. It is not a new classifier or evidence authority.
3. **Expose three modules:** Observed habit, Recurring situation and Rehearsal result. Workflow
   presets decide which modules appear; learners configure modules/assistance distance, not raw
   producer switches.
4. **Keep description separate from advice.** 1.0 cards state what was observed and offer exact
   replay/retry/theory/drill actions. They do not rank weaknesses, prescribe a training priority or
   call a correlation causal.
5. **Deterministic first, optional LLM wording.** Runtime joins an immutable local knowledge bundle
   and exact applicability. The LLM receives a sealed admitted card and may alter tone/obtuseness;
   it cannot retrieve, select, diagnose, grade or add chess claims.
6. **Privacy by construction.** Observation/profile data is opt-in for imports, exportable,
   deletable and version-recomputable under O13/F12. Sharing defaults to off and renders only the
   explicitly selected measured cards.
7. **Admission posture:** expose a metric only in populations/windows its measured floor supports;
   otherwise abstain. Treat R12's concentrated-blitz floors as prototype evidence, not universal
   defaults. Owner use may reject module UX; it cannot manufacture longitudinal transfer.

## Consequence of approval

O9 becomes answered for architecture and F9 may draft the ledger, module contracts, source/action
closure, privacy lifecycle and conservative admission/abstention behavior. F9 may not choose a
“most important weakness” formula, invent archetypes, implement semantic priority ranking, or own
F7's theory/drill applicability.
