## Procedure

### A. Baseline loop and R8

For each pack, the owner must:

1. commit a move without pre-commit evaluation;
2. play the declared consequence to its boundary;
3. rewind and preserve a second attempt;
4. compare the two attempts through the existing comparison surface;
5. replay one selected branch under a changed permitted resistance when available.

Do not explain the campaign pitch during the first pack. After each pack record: time to first
commit, consequence boundary, rewind, second branch, compare and replay; any abandoned action;
whether the owner asks for raw analysis; and the owner's verbatim answer to “what was useful here?”

R8 supports an O10 **continue** choice only if all three packs reach comparison without interaction
failure, the owner elects to start the next act after the prior one, and the final answer identifies
a value distinct from “show me the engine answer.” Any desire to stop, skip consequence play or use
rewind as erase is evidence against wrapping and must remain visible. A positive owner gate still
does not establish learning effect.

### B. R6 denial probe

During the second pack, after two completed alternative branches, present one disposable denial:
“This campaign version would spend its last retry here. Continue the standing branch or submit the
encounter.” Do not delete a branch or alter the run; this is a facilitator overlay, not product state.
Immediately record:

- whether the owner abandons the intended experiment;
- whether they switch to a safer line merely to conserve the count;
- whether the denial feels like a meaningful stake, an arbitrary tax or a broken promise;
- whether a budget on *looking/modules* would preserve the desired stake without pricing retry.

The retry-count candidate is refused if the owner says it prices the product's core curiosity, if
the planned branch is abandoned to conserve the count, or if the denial makes erase/engine-preview
more attractive. One clean failure is enough to refuse; one positive owner reaction only keeps the
candidate alive for participant testing.

### C. R7 ceiling probe

In the third pack, use the admitted R3 module packet in two counterbalanced moments:

- one moment where the encounter ceiling permits the known module;
- one where the same preset is selected but the ceiling suppresses that module and says why.

The suppressed state must offer a legal alternative action—play quietly, request an allowed form,
or open cited theory if permitted. It must not reveal what was withheld, raise the ceiling through
Customize or substitute an LLM claim.

Record whether the owner distinguishes `no relevant fact`, `provider unavailable` and `suppressed
by this encounter`; whether they understand that the preset did not change; whether they pursue an
alternative; and whether scarcity adds anticipation or only irritation.

The scarcity candidate fails if suppression is interpreted as a bug/unfairness after inspection,
if the owner cannot name why the module is absent, if Customize restores it, or if the owner exits
the encounter rather than playing. Passing this owner probe only opens a multi-participant tuning
study; it does not validate slot count, rarity or synergy.
