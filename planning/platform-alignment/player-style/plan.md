# R12 player-style metrics — predeclared plan

**Opened:** 2026-08-20

**Authority:** platform-alignment R12; disposable research only

**Status:** short-session arm answered 2026-08-20; longitudinal/cross-time-control transfer remains
unmeasured

## Question

Which descriptive player metrics are reproducible across disjoint games, inspectable to their
contributing decisions and sufficiently independent of rating to support a playful profile? This
pass does not diagnose weaknesses, prescribe training, match a learner to a grandmaster or assign
an archetype name.

## Source claims being tested

Chessiverse's current page simultaneously describes a preference quiz and an actual-game analyzer.
Its game-analysis section claims up to 100 games, 51 metrics, eight axes, 30+ archetypes, a 12-game
floor and ideally 50+ games. ChessBase's own follow-up says 100–200-game Style Reports can fluctuate
substantially and a more natural curve begins above 200. Neither publishes enough formulas to
reproduce its labels. The disagreement makes sample stability an experiment, not a copied default.

## Population

### Acquisition correction, before scoring

The initially planned per-user export route returned HTTP 404 for every tested account and for the
download URL generated by Lichess's own current profile form. No style metric had been computed or
inspected when this was found. R12 therefore replaces the acquisition mechanism, not its result,
with a frozen prefix of the official July 2026 standard-rated PGN. The Lichess database
documentation explicitly states that these Zstandard archives are partially decompressible. The
byte limit, source URL and compressed SHA-256 are frozen before scoring.

The first predeclared **268,435,456 compressed bytes** yielded 830,168 complete games over
00:00:32–10:06:02 UTC, including 335,807 eligible blitz games, but zero accounts reached the
200-game eligibility floor. No style metric was inspected. The final acquisition prefix is
therefore widened once, before scoring, to exactly **2,147,483,648 compressed bytes**. If this still
does not supply at least eight accounts in every rating band, R12 reports the shortfall and does not
widen or lower the floor again.

This is a chronological convenience prefix, not a representative player sample and not a
longitudinal sample. It can test whether a metric survives disjoint games within a concentrated
play window. It cannot establish stability across weeks, repertoire changes or different time
controls. R12 must report that external-validity arm as unmeasured rather than generalize from the
session window.

The already-captured 86 MiB decompressed discovery prefix contains 38,627 complete records from
00:00:32–00:32:25 UTC on July 1. It may be used only to estimate population availability and to
verify the parser; selection and scoring use the newly frozen larger prefix.

Candidate accounts are selected mechanically from the frozen prefix:

1. rated standard **blitz** games only, so time-control mix cannot masquerade as style;
2. at least 200 valid appearances in the prefix;
3. median observed rating inside one of 1000–1399, 1400–1799 or 1800–2199;
4. descending appearance count, then case-folded username;
5. public Lichess profile does not declare a bot;
6. take the first 12 accounts per band.

Retain each account's first 200 valid games in source order; a valid game has a result, standard
initial position, both ratings, at least ten full moves and a complete legal main line. Clock metrics
additionally require parseable `%clk` comments and a parseable base+increment. Raw public usernames
and games stay under `/private/tmp`; committed artifacts contain pseudonymous account ids, selection
rules, counts, digests and aggregate/bounded examples only.

If fewer than eight accounts survive in any band, report the shortfall and do not silently widen
the rating range or lower the game floor.

## Candidate metric registry

Every metric names its numerator, opportunity denominator and phase/color/time/rating scope. A
played-event rate alone is ineligible when the position determines whether that event was possible.

### Exact descriptive rates

1. `opening_surprisal` — mean `-log2(population move share)` through ply 8, only where the discovery
   prefix has at least 50 same-band observations at the exact position. This is population rarity,
   not creativity or theory knowledge.
2. `opening_family_entropy` — Shannon entropy over three-character ECO families, with number of
   games and effective-family count shown.
3. `fianchetto_setup_rate` — games reaching a bishop on b2/g2/b7/g7 behind its advanced b/g pawn.
4. `fianchetto_knight_screen_rate` — games reaching that setup with the same-side knight on the
   bishop's first inward diagonal square; no claim that the screen is good or intended.
5. `fianchetto_unblock_rate` — among moves from such screened positions, the fraction moving that
   knight and increasing the bishop's legal ray. This is the owner's configuration+consequence
   example in literal rule terms, not “likes discovered attacks.”
6. `castle_kingside_rate` / `castle_queenside_rate` — among games where that side still had a legal
   castling right at its first move, whether and where it castled; timing is reported separately.
7. `clock_spend_share` — at each decision,
   `max(0, previous_clock + increment - current_clock) / (previous_clock + increment)`, averaged by
   detected phase. A missing clock, a non-positive denominator or a current clock greater than the
   available clock abstains for that decision. The first decision starts from the declared base.

### Opportunity-adjusted choice residuals

For each eligible decision, compute `played_event - legal_alternative_event_share`; average the
residual, never the raw count. Legal alternatives include all promotion roles.

8. `pawn_choice_residual`;
9. `forcing_choice_residual` — capture or check;
10. `center_pawn_choice_residual` — pawn move ending on c4/d4/e4/f4/c5/d5/e5/f5;
11. `early_queen_choice_residual` — queen move before ply 16;
12. `nonpawn_capture_residual` — capture of a non-pawn outside a detected endgame. It is not called
    “simplifying”: a capture does not by itself establish that the position became simpler;
13. `opponent_reply_breadth_residual` — legal reply count after the played move minus its mean over
    legal alternatives, standardized per position. This is reply breadth, not “complexity.”

Phase uses the shipped Tabiya classifier and preserves its `unclear` abstention. Color-specific rows
are retained; pooled values weight colors equally so a one-color repertoire cannot dominate.

## Stability and separation tests

For each account, split the 200 games into alternating source-order games A/B. Repeat on nested
prefix sizes `{12, 25, 50, 100, 200}` using disjoint halves of size `floor(n/2)`. “Chronological” is
not claimed because equal-time ordering inside the export is not a stable identity.

A metric is mechanically retainable at sample size `n` only when:

- at least 75% of eligible accounts have a defined value in both halves;
- Spearman correlation across accounts is at least 0.70;
- median absolute A/B difference is no more than 0.15 on rate/residual metrics or 0.50 bits on
  entropy/surprisal;
- at least 75% of accounts stay on the same side of the same-band population median;
- the result holds in at least two of three rating bands and neither color reverses sign.

### Analysis correction after the first table

The predeclared “earliest passing `n`” rule produced a logically invalid floor: queenside castling
passed at 12, failed at 25 and passed later, while forcing choice passed at 50 and failed at both 100
and 200. The full predeclared pass table remains reported. Production eligibility uses the earliest
`n` that passes **and for which every larger measured sample also passes**. This persistence rule was
added after seeing the pass topology, so the dossier labels it a correction/sensitivity result, not
as predeclared confirmation. Failure at 200 refuses that metric from a 1.0 player profile; it does
not prove the human behavior is absent.

Uncertainty is shown from game-level bootstrap intervals for the retained raw metric. The UI
candidate must expose `games`, eligible decisions, window and reference population; it may not show
a naked percentile.

## Vector and archetype tests

Only individually retained metrics enter a standardized player vector. Rating-band and time-control
means are removed before vector tests.

1. **Self re-identification:** nearest cosine neighbor from half A among all half-B vectors must be
   the same account for at least 60% of accounts at the retained sample floor. This establishes a
   reproducible individual signature, not an interpretable chess “type.”
2. **Strength leakage:** five-fold cross-validated ridge regression (`lambda = 1`, folds assigned by
   pseudonymous account id modulo five) from the vector to the account's **within-band rating
   residual** must have `R² <= 0.10`. Predicting absolute rating after removing the band from the
   features would make the test easier for the wrong reason. If residual `R²` exceeds the gate,
   residualization is insufficient and the vector is a disguised skill measure.
3. **Cluster stability:** for k=4–12, bootstrap accounts/games and compare k-means assignments with
   adjusted Rand index over 100 resamples, with k-means++ seed `12082026` and 100 iterations. A
   canonical archetype count is refused unless median ARI is at least 0.70 and no cluster has fewer
   than 5% of accounts.

No cluster receives a chess name in this pass. A grandmaster match requires a separately licensed,
dated reference-game corpus processed through the identical registry and an owner ruling on the
presentation. Nearest-vector distance and uncertainty must remain visible.

## Controls and refusals

- Synthetic PGNs prove color, castling, fianchetto screen/unblock, promotion, check/capture and
  clock arithmetic.
- Shuffling account identity is a negative control for self re-identification.
- Rating alone is a positive control for the leakage test and must fail it.
- Outcome, accuracy and engine loss do not enter style. They remain rating/review objects.
- A game reaching an endgame does not prove the learner sought one; that vendor axis is refused
  until an opportunity-adjusted transition definition exists.
- “Risk,” “aggression,” “positional,” “tactical,” “patient,” “creative” and “theoretical” are not
  emitted by these atoms. They require an explicit, validated composition and cannot be inferred by
  an LLM.
- Longitudinal advice belongs to R13 and remains blocked even if a descriptive metric is stable.

## Exit

R12 completes when the population and all candidate metrics report pass/fail by sample size, every
retained row drills down to exact games/decisions, vector leakage/re-identification and cluster
stability are measured, and `design/research/player-style-metrics.md` records what may proceed.
Production remains blocked on O2/O9 and F1/F2/F9.
