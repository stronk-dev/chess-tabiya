## Blocking findings

### D2157 — named-structure identity still falls out of the sealed operand

The live named-structure projection admits only `provenanceNote`. Checkpoint P changes
`StructureMatch` to retain `id,name,provenanceNote,squares`, but its one declared operation edits
only `structure.ts`; it does not change the catalogue operands. The exact adapter row remains
`retained: ["provenanceNote"]` while targeting both `enum_state` and `square_set`. Under §2.2,
neither identity nor squares may be read because `sourceOperands` must occur in the projection's
literal operands.

Checkpoint P must amend the projection payload/operands and exact adapter row together, preserve a
typed structure identity and its witness squares, and name the label vocabulary that turns that
identity into learner text.

### D2158 — the citation component has no constructible source operand

`evidence_ref_resolution` retains only `{reference,text,sourceLabel}` but its composition includes
`citation`, whose required source is `{source,title,locator,licence,url?,revision?}` plus a bound
content digest. A pair-local adapter over the resolution projection cannot obtain those fields.
The current runtime may carry a separate provider evidence item in the same consumer view, but the
RFC explicitly seals adapters by exact projection+consumer and hands each constructor only that
projection's retained payload.

Either publish a derived, owner-bound citation projection containing the complete attribution
receipt, or specify and seal an exact multi-input derivation. A generic sibling-item lookup or a
fabricated label is refused.

### D2159 — deterministic templates bypass the total label registry

The author plan interpolates `{checkpointId}`, `{from}`, `{to}` and `{outcome}` directly into
learner prose. No renderer row names a typed vocabulary or label formatter. This reproduces the raw
id defect one layer inside `fact_statement`: objective states and outcomes can become ordinary
underscored/internal bytes while every renderer-set equality stays green.

Renderer variants must declare typed operands and total label/notation formatters. Opaque
checkpoint ids need an authored/registered display label or must remain technical metadata rather
than sentence text.

### D2160 — consequence rendering is not total over its repaired union

All three `run.record.consequence@1` adapter rows retain the terminal/nonterminal union, but only
`guidance.voice_compare@1` has `terminal=true` and `terminal=false` variants.
`guidance.voice_story@1` and `review.story@1` define terminal-only templates. The compiled binding
does not carry a payload predicate, so a nonterminal consequence is admitted and reaches a renderer
with no legal variant.

Give every consumer a total variant partition, or add an exact checked eligibility predicate that
makes the excluded arm unadmittable before presentation.

### D2161 — abstention authority neither preserves source reasons nor matches its RFC type

The RFC's `AbstentionOperand` carries question, projection and producer identity. The executable
`PresentationAbstentionLifecycle` carries none of them. Its per-adapter plan maps whole families to
generic reason lists without declaring how real source reasons—including
`outside_tablebase_domain`, `empty_population`, `model_failure`, `input_abstained` and
`no_recorded_trail`—map into the six learner reasons. Thus provider absence, outside-domain refusal
and genuine empty can collapse while D2139's current test remains green.

Make the executable type identical to the RFC authority and publish a total, source-specific
reason mapping joined to each projection's declared abstention set. Missing, extra and many-to-one
collapses that lose a learner-relevant distinction must fail.

### D2162 — silent authored claims are forced to render absence cards

§3.10 deliberately declares `claim` empty behavior `silent` because saying “the author said
nothing” at every position is alert fatigue. The plan nevertheless puts all four claim adapters in
`PRESENTATION_ABSTENTION_ROWS`, and criteria 21b require every non-removal adapter there. §4 also
says an absent component does not disappear. These rules cannot all be implemented.

Define the abstention population from declared empty behavior: `silent` produces no component;
`stated_absence` and `unavailable_source` require typed receipts. Keep source failure visible only
where the owning workflow actually asked the question.

### D2163 — `count_with_denominator` can complete with zero consumers

The component is one of the closed fourteen, but none of the 112 exact adapter targets uses it and
no discharge assigns a future binding. Synthetic five-state component tests therefore let it count
as complete without a production path—the registry-only completion shape this RFC rejects.

Bind it to exact real count/coverage operands at checkpoint A or B, or remove it from 1.0 and route
its named future owner. Every required component needs at least one reachable adapter/seat before
the RFC can archive.
