- the source-attribution digest covers the canonical id, version, resolver, policy and row image;
- a citation cannot be constructed without non-empty revision and licence metadata;
- only operations with a real terminal abstention arm produce an honest-empty row, and their
  exported result discriminants—not presentation defaults—own its exact reasons;
- registered questions are canonical process objects whose authority is lost by spread, clone or
  serialization;
- structure match and witness geometry traverse one exported predicate expression; and
- Explorer candidate and committed-edge identity is canonical UCI, with SAN retained only for
  display.

`empty_population | source_unavailable`. Checkpoint P now has an eighth exact operation that
exports and joins the real server vocabulary. The disposable test reads the `CorpusResult` union
from `apps/server/src/corpus.ts`, so a future source/plan mismatch fails independently of the
plan's own declarations.

`make evidence-presentation-fifth-author-repair` passes all retained author layers and the new
repair: 31 runtime assertions plus lifecycle typechecking.
