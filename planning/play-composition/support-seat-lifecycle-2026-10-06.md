## Repair

One scalar authority binds run, current recorded decision, finalized assistance,
compilation readiness, access role and preview. Its change retires every pending
and completed seat, not merely entries already present in `seatSubjects`. Retired
success/error is refused before parsing; destruction also retires staged generations.
Identical snapshots preserve the admitted page, badge and explicit expansion.

Before admission, the response must match the captured decision, timing, requested
help, square, candidate and generation. A genuine older sealed page is not proof
that it answers the current request. On-request seats return to their explicit
door, never silently re-request after navigation or a help change. Proactive seats
and a still-selected square refresh under current authority; the existing rule
against retroactive delivery when raising a preset remains. A square refresh does
not close another explicitly selected card. Old marks/focus operands are retired.

A stale empty staged check cannot auto-commit after navigation or destruction.
Mismatched decision/candidate/generation is unavailable, not safe. Explicit
confirmation still commits the exact legal candidate once; revise or a changed
position cancels it. No chess verdict or source payload is manufactured.
