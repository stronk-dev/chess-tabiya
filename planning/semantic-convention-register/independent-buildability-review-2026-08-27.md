## Blocker 1: the collision key does not serialize one convention lineage ([[D1917]])

The draft keys collision identity by the complete member ref. Two active RFCs may therefore claim
`space@2` and `space@3` concurrently. They are disjoint under C3 even though the meaning of v3
necessarily depends on which v2 definition becomes its predecessor. They may land in reverse order
and the membership set has no per-id head with which to reject either landing.

Key live ownership by base convention id. For a new id, require `@1`; for an existing id, derive its
highest landed version and require exactly head+1. Permit multiple disjoint **ids**, not multiple
versions of one id. Keep every prior version in the landed/tree sets as historical readable state.
Add crossed fixtures for `space@2`/`space@3`, two `space@2` claimants, two different ids, skipped
versions and a valid next version.

## Blocker 2: member equality cannot observe semantic-byte drift ([[D1918]])

The proposed TypeChecker projects each declaration to only `id@version`. A mutation of definition,
limitations, authority and disclosure that retains the ref produces the same tree set and the same
README rows. C10 therefore cannot establish its stated “same-version replacement” guarantee.

Either narrow this process RFC explicitly to identity membership and route all semantic-byte
immutability to the product RFC's independently checked previous-release authority, or register a
semantic digest here. Do not leave a criterion claiming that member equality sees bytes it erases.
The later product review must separately prove that its previous-release snapshot cannot be edited
in the same change to bless a rewrite.

## Blocker 3: the initial seed has no durable machine authority ([[D1919]])

The only executable 39-member source is `INITIAL_CONVENTION_MEMBERS`, a non-exported constant in a
file whose first line labels it a disposable research harness. The process RFC requires C10 to
compare against that set, but specifies neither a parser nor a durable seed file, excludes such a
file from its implementation boundary, and already repeats the literal population in two RFCs.

Publish one stable machine-readable seed artifact and have both the D1722 census test and C10 read
it. Include that artifact in the implementation boundary and make deletion/drift fail. Do not copy
the 39 refs into register-check or another test.

the original shared-register RFC plus the later campaign schema. Semantic conventions become the
eighth. Correct this draft's “seventh” wording and the product RFC's claim that the current grammar
knows only six. This does not require an owner ruling.
