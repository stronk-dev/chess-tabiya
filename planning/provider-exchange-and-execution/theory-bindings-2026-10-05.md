instead of compiling only the Explorer projection. Missing recorded claims or online
population facts omit their own optional input; matched local theory remains usable.

## Production behavior

RunService queries finalized module demand first. Quiet, unrequested and withheld queries
do not compile a provider operation or acquire a page. Admitted Theory demand compiles
all four bindings, including their transitive provider requirements, literal absence
policies and latency limits. The exact population binding must admit the registered
Explorer operation and optional omission before acquisition can proceed.

A contract failure is not a provider absence: missing policies or impossible binding
latency prevent I/O. Configured-source absence, sparse/zero pages and exact-request
admission retain their existing behavior. Post-I/O authorization, disclosure and decision
checks remain intact, and neither raw candidate rows nor an observed-move substitution
enters the Theory receipt.
