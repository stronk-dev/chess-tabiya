# D3431 — output-preserving longitudinal prefix reuse

Implementation correction under owner-directed `rfc/longitudinal-store.md` §A. This is not
a narrower collector, a new authority, or a derivation revision. The unchanged local closure
produces genuine sealed events; only its complete v1 projection/sign Boolean memberships are
retained, for the exact before-FEN/canonical-move/after-FEN tuple.

## Production boundary

`longitudinal-projector.ts` uses one process/thread-local reader only for the frozen production
dependencies. Its 4,096-entry FIFO stores immutable string arrays, including successful empty
results; thrown/undefined results are not retained. Injected dependencies are always recollected.
Both six-field FENs survive key construction. Every decision still obtains its complete legal
population, including the committed edge, and receives a fresh membership Set. Source replay,
ownership/attribution, canonical decision refs, phase/class denominators, row accumulation and
worker claim/publication authority remain live. Every synchronous progress checkpoint still runs.
No run, learner, source image, population verdict or durable row is cached.

The bound applies to entries, not an assumed byte size. Process restart, FIFO pressure or a new
edge can cause recollection. Cold unseen-game cost and D3300's sub-second goal are not discharged.
No collector inventory, registry artifact, version, revision, schema or migration changes.
