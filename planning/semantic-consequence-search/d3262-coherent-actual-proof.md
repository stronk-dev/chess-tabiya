nonempty legal defence set. An executed strategy additionally needs an actual

counterexample or absorbing terminal for **every legal preparation**, not merely
every selected one. Missing branches remain explicit unknowns. Automatic terminal
outcomes never create vacuous universals and retain physical legal moves separately.
