## Identity, arithmetic and abstention

The observer tracks the named attacker/victim or minor/destination across every
actual legal move, including controller capture, en passant, rook relocation on
castling and promoted role. A destination's controlling pawn is a post-candidate
identity: another candidate cannot inherit its existence. Capturing that controller
does not discard the surviving minor. Lost target identities stay lost; terminal
positions cannot acquire a fabricated continuation.

Opportunity mass is counted **once per third-ply predecessor**, using its original
two-layer conditional mass. Execution mass is counted only for selected fourth-ply
leaves executing the target, using the original three-layer mass. The arithmetic
control deliberately gives one 0.25 predecessor two leaves (0.15 execution / 0.10
other): opportunity stays 0.25, not 0.50, and execution stays 0.15, not 0.25.
No selected frontier is renormalized. Residuals and covered predecessor/final masses
come from the already checked model frontier.

All 364 target-arm cells retain `abstain_from_partial_frontier` for negative proof
and `not_evaluated` for universal proof. The 42/39 missed possibilities are **not**
prevention verdicts. High local policy coverage cannot justify a universal negative.
The model also supplies the learner replies; this is not arbitrary-defence coverage.
