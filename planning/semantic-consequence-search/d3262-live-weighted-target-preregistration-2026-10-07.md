## Projection and non-vacuity

- Opportunity mass: sum the joint mass of each visited three-ply predecessor
  with the named opportunity **once**, not once per fourth-ply continuation.
- Execution mass: sum joint mass of each selected fourth-ply leaf that actually
  executes that named target action. Execution requires an opportunity at its
  exact predecessor, not a similarly labelled target elsewhere.
- Reintroduced opportunity/execution masses are the same sums restricted to
  targets removed by the candidate and available again at the predecessor.
- All event identities bind to selected policy histories and their literal joint
  masses. A missing, extra, duplicate, crossed or resealed event/weight refuses.
- Covered policy layer mass stays separate from event mass; absorbed earlier
  terminal paths retain policy mass without invented target continuations.
- Failed/incomplete source cells retain observations/omissions and unknown
  complete event mass. They never become zero coverage, zero probability or a
  negative target verdict. Two-ply cells do not borrow four-ply target values.
- Use configured model mass only. It is not measured human frequency, a probability
  that an arbitrary learner chooses the model's move, engine causality or a proof
  over every legal defence. No normalization to one or successful-path filtering.

Report event-identity changes separately from weighted-value changes and literal
aggregate-addition differences. Preserve the earlier reported aggregate values
beside independently recompiled event sums; a floating-point ordering difference
is not a changed chess outcome or a threshold decision.

Positive controls must have one opportunity predecessor with multiple selected
leaves (to expose double counting), a non-executing leaf, two targets with different
event masks on one shared policy, changed weights without changed masks, and early
terminal absorption. Negative controls include execution without its own opportunity,
missing/crossed history weights, Boolean/type corruption, duplicate paths, policy
mass substituted for event mass, target cross, lost whole cases, and partial mass
coerced to zero. Pure controls are synthetic algebra, not new chess evidence.
