1. At each selected ply-2 preparation, enumerate **every legal learner defence**.
   Track the original named pieces through castling, en passant, capture and
   promotion. Compute a fixed relation signature, not generic operand contact:
   material actor/target live identities and geometric actor→target attack;
   destination minor identity, geometric minor→destination attack, destination
   occupant color/role, declared controller identity and geometric pawn control.
   The defender event set consists of moves changing these truth/occupancy fields
   or removing an identity. Coordinate changes alone are not events. These are
   geometric scheduling operands, not legality of hypothetical off-turn attacks,
   exchange profit, tactical labels, survival proofs or strategic evaluation.
2. At each selected ply-3 predecessor, enumerate every legal opponent action.
   The event set is the original live named actor capturing the original live
   target, or the original live minor arriving on the registered square. Any
   legal promotion choices remain separate. A geometric event is not asserted

3. At either node, begin with the same-budget captured top-eight engine table's
   top `width` moves. Reserve **one** event: first among retained engine ranks

   Fill/cardinality stay exactly `min(width, legalCount)`. This explicit unranked
   tie convention does not pretend an all-legal provider ordering exists. Scores
