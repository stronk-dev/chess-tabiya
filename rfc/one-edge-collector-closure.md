## Summary

Make one-edge completeness describe collectors that actually run. Remove the two-move
recorded-trade relation from the candidate's declared event image and selector coverage;
preserve recorded-trade support and all remaining source behavior.

## Problem and evidence

The candidate generator includes every exchange event declaration, including
`derived.exchange.trade_completed@1`. Actual `localSemanticEventClosure` calls capture-class,
not the separate trade factory requiring two captures and recorded move anchors. The executed
probe measures seven legal members per color, available trade coverage and zero trade factory
calls. A genuine two-edge recorded @2 window emits one trade per color. Profile's current
54-source image already excludes the one-edge trade slot. This is an overstated acquisition
claim, not an emitted false trade and not missing chess capability.

## 1. Exact scope correction

The unit is an exact projection reference. Candidate event coverage and played ranking modes
contain **41** references: the former 42 minus exactly `derived.exchange.trade_completed@1`.
Selector accepts, binding eligibility and result coverage contain these 41 plus the existing
13 condition references, exactly **54**. All other refs, versions and their order are retained.
The exchange generator group contains only `derived.exchange.capture_class@1`, resolved through
its existing declaration. Use an explicit collector composition at that group, as other groups
already do; registration in the global exchange list does not imply candidate acquisition.
Remove the unreachable trade ranking-mode entry. No generic suffix heuristic may stand in
for proving this collector's actual composition.

Keep the global trade declarations, original recorded trade factories, modern recorded-window
@2 route, their consumers and historical validation records. No invented recapture, recorded
move anchor, unavailable cause or available-empty result is introduced. Existing original root
and child evidence remains authoritative. All legal moves and denominators stay unchanged.

Profile's existing54 machine refs and longitudinal ingest registry's local41/condition13/
recorded-deferred11 split remain unchanged. This RFC makes the selector agree with that scope;
it does not admit recorded trade into longitudinal statistics or remove recorded Review support.
Do not rewrite immutable registry artifacts or manufacture another source register.

## 2. Versions and integration

Keep compiler7 and selector/consumer3 for this prerelease correctness repair. The selected
result remains process-owned and unrestorable. No valid trade output, chess operand, predicate,
denominator, threshold or ranking order changes; only an unreachable admission slot is removed.
The manifest's changed accepts/bindings change its digest, already part of the original packet
identity. The application service is process-local and keyed by FEN, not manifest digest; its
entries cannot survive a process/code replacement. Prove original packet identity/refusal and
that process boundary rather than add cache generations or aliases. There is no
schema/migration/derivation-revision allocation here. D4200's existing revision10 and next
migration obligations remain owned by its parent and must use the corrected54 source scope.

Regenerate the candidate projection file with its existing generator. Correct current exact
scope assertions and operational preflight to54. Preserve retired versions' rejection controls.
Refresh current source-validation receipts using the actual implementation; inherited historical
receipts never establish correctness of changed source scope. No qualification promotion follows.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A genuine one-edge fixture in both colors acquires capture-class but never trade. Its current
   selector coverage/accepts/bindings/modes exclude trade; the original55 implementation fails
   these assertions. The separate two-capture recorded window still produces authentic trade@2.
2. Generated local refs equal the existing longitudinal ingest registry's41 native-plus-edge refs in exact
   identity (compare sets, preserving each owner's declared order). Selector54 equals the existing
   Profile54 set. Separately compare before/after order: deletion of trade is the sole difference
   in the candidate/selector images. A count alone is insufficient.
3. All remaining available/unknown source classifications, selected fact identities/order,
   operands, support shares and legal denominators agree on the genuine complete and partial
   controls. Missing required root risk remains unknown; unrelated legacy failures stay local.
   Unknown source/refusal, getter/context-mixing and execution-preflight controls remain intact.
4. Changing the manifest changes the packet ID; current original-receipt checks reject foreign
   or reconstructed receipts. The service remains confined to one process and its immutable
   PRIMARY manifest, with no external receipt insertion or in-process manifest swap. No cache
   invalidation mechanism is claimed or added. Recorded factories/registry/Profile scope and
   historical files are unchanged.
5. Current canonical validation retains1470 case values/order,89 subjects and17 qualified profiles;
   regenerate current receipts/census and explain any difference instead of editing expectations
   to hide it. No new qualification or ordinary26 adoption is claimed.
6. Focused runtime/server/generator/probe tests, typecheck/build, normal `make verify-software`
   and `make verify-governance` pass. Ledger, roadmap and append-only log close only this bounded
   correction; parent storage, consumer, content and release obligations stay open.
