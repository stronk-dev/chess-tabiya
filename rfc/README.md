reservation; it is a hole that the next migration to land will seal shut. Claude created
exactly that hazard on 2026-08-16 by telling `board-annotation` to claim **23** while **22**

its turn*, not on the integer printed in it. **An implementer who finds the next contiguous
number already taken renegotiates here rather than skipping.** The earlier 21/22 reassignment
was sound for the same reason — the draft that could not land is the one that moved.

documents are one layer apart: a seat decides *what may appear and how much of it*; a component
decides *what shape a typed operand takes once it is there*. Neither may specify the other's
half, and `evidence-presentation.md` criterion 18 is the grep-able fence (no `EvidenceForm`
member, no `ModuleDeclaration` field, no seat id, no `AnswerDistance` member, no
`evidence-catalog.ts` edit).

**The join is one-way and by type only**, so **landing order is free in either direction**: a
component declares which `EvidenceForm` members it may serve, under the mapping
`rfc/learner-modules.md` §1.12 already pins; nothing in `evidence-catalog.ts` imports
`presentation-contract.ts`. Both documents consume `learner-modules.md`'s shipped `forms`,
`budgets` and `emptyBehavior` fields and **neither re-decides them**.

Two shared client files are named rather than left to collide. **`DrillScreen.svelte` is touched
by both**: the seat scaffolding and its budget plumbing are `module-registration`'s; the leaf
renderers it replaces are `evidence-presentation`'s (`:385` the pivotal enum label, `:414-419`
and `:1150-1151` the human-split distribution and its orphaned convention, `:1157` the corpus
sentence list, `:381-383` the square-set expansion). No line is claimed twice. **`App.svelte`,
`AssistanceSettings.svelte`, `CompareView.svelte`, `WhyBanner.svelte`, `Timeline.svelte`,
`PackList.svelte`, `RatingScreen.svelte`, `GroupPanel.svelte`, `GameStoryScreen.svelte` and
`corpus-sentences.ts` are `evidence-presentation`'s alone** — they carry the raw dumps [[D1434]]
names and no seat sits on any of them.

One measured denominator is re-derived rather than copied ([[D1381]]'s second clause):
[[D1431]]'s **42 specified affordances / 17 exist / 8 designed** is that row's measurement and
`evidence-presentation.md` cites it as such, asserting nothing on it. Its own baselines — 43
id/enum text sites, 11 de-underscore sites, 16 hard-coded colour declarations — are derived by
its own §8 commands at `f0d5460` and are declared drift tripwires, never criteria ([[D1240]]).

Pin, 2026-08-26: **`social-play.md` owns run-schema lane 0.23 (`game.resigned`, `draw.agreed`) and
`enforced-clocks.md` owns lane 0.21 (`clock.flagged`); `native-ratings.md` CONSUMES both and opens no
lane of its own.** Those three events are the run-side of the declared result and their owners are the
two RFCs that derived the `events.ts:330-333` / `:344-345` mechanism; `native-ratings.md` adds only the
object they feed — `games.result_cause` — and its criterion 10 **fails** if a `game.resigned`,
`draw.agreed` or `clock.flagged` member is added in its own change set instead of theirs, so the pin is
tested rather than trusted. The migration chain carries the same relationship in the opposite direction:
`enforced-clocks` claims a rebuild of `rated_games.terminal_reason` and `native-ratings` **deletes that
table**, so `native-ratings` takes the **tail** position deliberately — landing last is what keeps both
migration bodies correct on a database that never reached either. `native-ratings`' body is written to
survive the other order anyway, mapping `terminal_reason` through a frozen seven-member table covering
migration 25's five values and `enforced-clocks`' two additions. `social-play.md` §3.6 and
`native-ratings.md` §5 also touch the same forcing at `service.ts:2134-2136` in **disjoint halves**:
social-play removes it, native-ratings supplies the guest's attempt row without which removing it counts
the game for the host alone. Neither claims the other's bytes; the amendment social-play owes is that
RFC's Discharge D2 in `native-ratings.md`.
